# Beginner Hiking Hub: Trail Essentials & Safety

## Welcome to the Trail
Starting your hiking journey is a rewarding way to connect with nature, stay active, and explore new landscapes. This guide provides essential safety tips, recommended gear, and reliable resources to ensure your first hike is both safe and enjoyable.

## What to Bring on a Short Hike
* **Hydration**: At least 16 to 24 ounces of water per hour of moderate hiking.
* **Nutrition**: High-energy, lightweight snacks such as trail mix, granola bars, or dried fruit.
* **Safety & Navigation**: A fully charged mobile phone, a portable power bank, and a downloaded digital or physical trail map.
* **First Aid**: Basic adhesive bandages, antiseptic wipes, and any necessary personal medications.
* **Layers**: Moisture-wicking base layers and a lightweight, windproof or waterproof outer shell depending on weather conditions.

## Essential Trail Etiquette & Safety Rules
* **Stay on the Marked Path**: Protect local ecosystems, prevent erosion, and avoid getting lost by sticking strictly to designated trails.
* **Leave No Trace**: Pack out everything you bring in, including food wrappers, packaging, and waste.
* **Yield to Others**: Hikers traveling uphill generally have the right-of-way. Step aside safely to let faster traffic or uphill hikers pass when appropriate.

## Official Resources & Further Reading
* Plan your routes and check current trail conditions through official federal resources like the [USDA Forest Service Hiking Safety Guide](https://www.fs.usda.gov).
* Review comprehensive outdoor ethics and leave-no-trace principles at [Leave No Trace Center for Outdoor Ethics](https://lnt.org).
